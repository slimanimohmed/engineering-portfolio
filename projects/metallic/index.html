THE ENGINEERING TASK

What needed
to be solved.

The design brings together the supporting columns, braces, platform, roof, stair flights, landing and safety railings within one coordinated structural layout.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I developed a three-dimensional CAD layout of a raised steel platform with a roof and an external access staircase.

  1. 01

    I modeled the main structural arrangement, including supports and bracing.

  2. 02

    I integrated the raised platform, stairs, landing and railings into the CAD assembly.

  3. 03

    I reviewed the arrangement and component interfaces in an isometric view.
