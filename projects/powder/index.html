THE ENGINEERING TASK

What needed
to be solved.

Making parts and experimental devices work in practice requires more than a CAD model. It means preparing manufacturable geometry, selecting appropriate processes, checking equipment and refining the final part.

MY CONTRIBUTION

At the UEMF Advanced Manufacturing Lab, I connected mechanical design with practical fabrication: a powder-flowability apparatus, mold preparation, CNC work, additive prototyping and workshop maintenance.

  1. 01

    Developed a powder-flowability apparatus using ASTM B213 as a design reference, connecting an engineering drawing with a physical funnel-and-cup setup.

  2. 02

    Prepared CAD and Fusion 360 CAM work for tooling and machining, including mold geometry and logo machining.

  3. 03

    Worked with CNC machining, part finishing, manual layout and dimensional checks across different workshop projects.

  4. 04

    Gained practical exposure to FDM/SLS prototyping and SLS post-processing, alongside instrumentation and preventive maintenance. Broader additive-manufacturing learning also included SLM/LPBF process exposure.
